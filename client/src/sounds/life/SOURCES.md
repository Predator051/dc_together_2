# Living things outside: where the recordings come from

All from freesound.org, licence **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication; no attribution required, given anyway).
Taken from the site's 128 kbit/s previews, cut to short calls, made mono, cleaned of rumble (high-pass) and,
for the noisy ones, of hiss (FFT denoise), peaks set to −3 dBFS, MP3 64 kbit/s, 32 kHz. Played by `client/src/life.ts`.
